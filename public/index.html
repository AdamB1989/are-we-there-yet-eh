September 8, 2026 — Canadian counter-tariffs

Canada's Department of Finance says Canada imposed counter-tariffs of 15%, 25%, and 50% on $27.6 billion of U.S. imports after the United States imposed a 50% tariff on $27.6 billion of Canadian goods effective August 22.
